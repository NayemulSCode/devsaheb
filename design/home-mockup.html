how we work

Five stages. Nothing skipped when the schedule tightens.

01

Discover

We map the real constraint before proposing anything. Usually it isn't the one in the brief.

02

Architect

Schema, boundaries, and failure modes on paper, approved by you before code exists.

03

Build

Two-week increments. Every PR reviewed by a second engineer, CI green before merge.

04

Harden

Load tests, accessibility audit, performance budget enforced, penetration pass.

05

Ship & support

Monitoring, on-call rotation, and a documented handover your own team can act on.
